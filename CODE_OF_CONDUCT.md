# Code of conduct

## 1 Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, critique work rather than people, and make technical disagreements evidence-based. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## 2 Accessibility and safety

This project handles media that may be unsafe for photosensitive viewers. Clearly label hazardous fixtures, avoid embedding autoplaying flashes in discussions, and prefer static or text evidence when reporting an issue.

## 3 Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens people, the project, or its users.
